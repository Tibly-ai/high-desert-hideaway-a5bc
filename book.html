Before you book

House rules, plainly stated

01

How many nights minimum?

Two nights most of the year, three over Thanksgiving, Christmas, New Year's and the long spring weekends when Joshua Tree fills up. Single nights open up occasionally between bookings. Send us a note and we will look.

02

Can we bring the dog?

Yes. Two dogs, $25 each per stay. The yard is fenced on three sides and open desert on the fourth, so keep an eye out at night. There are bowls, towels and a hose by the back step.

03

Who fits, and where do they sleep?

Four guests. One queen in the front bedroom, one queen in the back. No sofa bed, no air mattresses, no extra bodies past four. It keeps the septic and the neighbors happy.

04

Is the cowboy pool filled?

March through October it is filled, treated and ready. In winter we drain it, because desert nights drop below freezing and a stock tank of ice is nobody's idea of a soak.

05

Fire pit and firewood?

First bundle is on us, stacked under the ramada. More is available in town at the hardware store on Twentynine Palms Highway. Fires go out before bed, and we shut it down entirely on red flag wind days.

06

What if plans change?

Cancel 14 days out for a full refund. Inside 14 days we refund whatever we can rebook. Weather closures at the park or a road washout, we work it out with you. Ranch rules, not fine print.
